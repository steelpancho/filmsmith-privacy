FilmSmith Privacy Policy

Updated November 6, 2026

FilmSmith is a shot planner for filmmakers. This page covers FilmSmith for iPhone, iPad, and Mac, including the test copies sent through Apple's TestFlight.

The short version: FilmSmith has no account and no server. Your projects, photos, and settings stay on your device. I don't collect, see, sell, or share any of it, and nothing in FilmSmith tracks you.

What stays on your device

  • Your projects: scripts, scenes, shots, frames and other photos, locations, shoot days, people, gear, and settings, saved only in FilmSmith's own storage on your device.
  • Keys for AI services, in your device's Keychain, or on a Mac in FilmSmith's private storage.
  • People you add from Contacts: only the details you pick are copied into your project.
  • Your location: only when you tap Current Location, to place a location, and then only in your project. The camera is used only when you take a photo.

What leaves your device, and only when you use it

  • Weather: for a shoot day with a location and a date, FilmSmith asks Open-Meteo for that place's forecast. The request has the location's coordinates and the dates, and nothing else. Open-Meteo's own privacy policy covers its servers.
  • Maps: place search, addresses, Look Around, and the nearest-hospital search use Apple Maps, under Apple's privacy policy.
  • AI frames and photos: when you generate one, FilmSmith sends your prompt and any reference pictures you chose to the AI service you connected (fal, Runway, Higgsfield, Google Gemini, OpenAI, or OpenRouter), with your own account and key. That service's privacy policy covers what it does with them. I never see them.
  • MCP Connection (Mac only): off until you switch it on. Then AI apps on the same Mac that you set up can read and change your projects. With Show Pictures to AI Apps on, they also see your frames, and send them to the company that makes the AI app. Those apps' privacy policies apply. When an AI app asks FilmSmith to add a picture from the web, FilmSmith downloads it from that address.
  • Sharing and backups: project files and backups go only where you send or save them.
  • Send Feedback: starts an email in your own mail app with your FilmSmith version, device model, and system version. You see it all before sending, nothing is sent until you do, and nothing from your projects is included.

TestFlight test copies

If you test FilmSmith through TestFlight, Apple shares with me what its TestFlight privacy information describes, such as your name and email if I invited you by email, which copy you installed, your device and system version, how often you open the app, crash reports, and the feedback you send through TestFlight. I use it only to fix and improve FilmSmith, and never share it.

Children

FilmSmith isn't made for children, and it doesn't collect anything from anyone.

Changes

If anything here changes, I'll update this page and its date.
